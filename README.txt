TIAMO MOHAMAD PORTFOLIO
=======================

1) Open index.html to preview.
2) Replace the six project placeholders in index.html with your images/videos later.
3) Replace the WhatsApp/social links in the CONTACT section.
4) Upload index.html, style.css and script.js to a GitHub repository.
5) GitHub: Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main > /(root) > Save.
6) Your site will be available at:
   https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/REPOSITORY-NAME/

Recommended repository name: tiamo-portfolio

No prices are included.
